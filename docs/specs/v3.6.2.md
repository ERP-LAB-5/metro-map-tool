## Goals

- The navigation board and the speech bubble agree on the date while the traveller waits at a stop.

## Features

### Board date while waiting (designer)

**From the parking lot:** "The navigation board's date while waiting at a stop".

**What:** While waiting at a stop, the board named that stop but showed the *next* stop's date,
while the bubble showed the stop's own. The board's date is now the date of the stop it names: the
stop it waits at, else the next stop. While waiting, the next stop's date follows its name:
`on Main · 2027-05-01 · then C (2027-09-01)`.

**Acceptance criteria:**
- [x] Waiting at a stop: the board's date is that stop's, the same as its bubble.
- [x] Waiting: "then" names the next stop with its date.
- [x] Moving: the date is the next stop's, with the countdown.
- [x] Arrived: the date is the last stop's.

Checked in headless Chrome at the start, waiting, moving and arrived points of a ride (no JS test
harness in the repo).
