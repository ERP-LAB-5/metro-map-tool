Stations

Place stations first. Drag a dot on the canvas to move it — it snaps to the grid.

    Junctions

    A bend with no platform: somewhere a line can split or rejoin without stopping. It never shows on the finished map.

      Lines

      Order matters: lines sharing a corridor are drawn as parallel tracks in this order.

        Zones

        A tinted band behind the stations you put in it — “Upgrade”, “Preferred”, and so on.

          Swimlanes

          A named band of rows across the whole map, one for each key area. A station belongs to the lane its row falls in, so dragging it into another band moves it there. Pick a lane, then click a station to stretch the lane over its row, or fit the lane to a line.

            Joins

            A stretched interchange: one capsule covering several stops, the way a tube map marks a correspondance. Pick a join, then click the stops it covers — their own markers give way to the capsule.

              Rides

              A traveller's journey: pick where it starts and ends — a station, a junction, or where a line runs off the map — and the track decides the way, changing line where it has to. Add a via to send it down a particular branch, and mark stops to ride straight through. ▶ Navigate follows one ride like a satnav.

                Timeline

                In roadmap mode the x axis is a calendar. A whole grid x is a period boundary; a station at 2.5 sits mid-period.

                Style

                Saved with the map, and used by the command line unless a flag overrides it.
